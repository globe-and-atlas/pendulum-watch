A little clockwork on your wrist.

Pendulum pairs a compact analog dial with a swinging seconds bob. The clock hands show hours and minutes; the bob can carry the seconds number.

Motion:
• Tick-tock mode alternates sides once per second.
• Optional smooth swing adds intermediate positions.
• Show or hide the seconds number inside the bob.

Make it yours:
• Six palettes: Night, Paper, Mint, Ice, Rose, and Monochrome.
• Custom background, text, hour-and-bob, and minute-hand colors.
• Watch default, 12-hour, or 24-hour numeric time.
• Optional digital time below the pendulum.
• Optional date: YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY, or DD/MM/YYYY.

Designed for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2. Settings are available through the Pebble app. No phone service is needed for timekeeping.

The first gallery image demonstrates the smooth-swing option in six palettes. The installed face defaults to tick-tock motion and keeps the palette you choose.
